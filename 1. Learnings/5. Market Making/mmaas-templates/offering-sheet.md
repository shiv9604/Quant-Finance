# Kosashi Capital: Market-Making Offering

What a client gets from Kosashi Capital. This is the positive, client-facing summary. Full terms are agreed in the term sheet.

## What we do
- Continuous two-sided quotes on your token, on the exchanges you choose
- Tight spreads and real depth close to the price, so investors can trade calmly
- A healthy market that keeps you in good standing with your exchanges

## Service levels we commit to
- Spread kept within the agreed target on each exchange
- Agreed depth on both sides within 2% of the price
- A 97%+ uptime target, with at least 95% guaranteed, measured every 10 seconds

## You stay in control
- Your tokens and stablecoins stay in your own exchange account
- We trade through a trade-only key, with withdrawals disabled and access locked to our servers
- You can see every trade and balance at any time

## Transparency
- Weekly and monthly performance reports
- Every target measured and shown, exchange by exchange

## Clean incentives
- A simple monthly retainer for market quality
- We earn nothing from your price going up or down
- Trading results stay in your account

## Integrity
- Organic trading only
- No wash trading, no artificial volume, no price targets
- Self-trade prevention always on

## Simple commercial terms
- Monthly retainer, paid at the start of each month
- 6 to 12 month engagements
- Confidential on both sides
