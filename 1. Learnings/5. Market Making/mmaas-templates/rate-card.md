# Kosashi Capital — Retainer Rate Card (internal, v2)

Set by the founder 2026-11-27 (MMaaS Layer 6). Prices in USDT/USDC, monthly in advance.
Unit = one **market** = one trading pair on one exchange.

## List prices (v2, founder 2026-11-27: simpler model)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Setup / onboarding fee | $5,000 one-off, every client |
| First pair on each exchange (supported or new; no separate integration fee) | $5,000 / month per exchange |
| Each extra pair on the same exchange | $3,000 / month |
| Premium SLA (e.g. 99% uptime, tighter spread) | +20–30% |
| Minimum term | 6 months (12 preferred) |

## Concessions (negotiation only, never upfront)

Founder rule 2026-11-27: list prices stay as above. The one advertised give is a setup-fee discount for a 12-month contract. Everything below is held back and only traded for something (longer term, case study, referral).


- Setup fee: may drop to $2,500 for a 12-month contract. Never fully free on a 6-month contract.
- Volume discount: may offer ~10–20% off the monthly total at 3+ exchanges (keeps us below tier-1 pricing).
- No token options or token payments at the start (founder 2026-11-27): cash-only retainer for simplicity. Revisit after the first clients.

## Introducers

- 5% of fees actually received (monthly + setup), pay as paid, first 12 months only.
- Written introducer agreement before introductions; introducer must disclose they are paid by us and confirm no role at the client.

## Economics check

Fixed cost ~$4,000/month (founder $2,500, team ~$1,000, base servers ~$500), plus ~$300–400 variable per market and 5% introducer fee where applicable.
One Kestrel-size client ($10k): ~50% margin. Two: ~70%. Three: ~78%. Re-check every quarter as fixed costs grow.
