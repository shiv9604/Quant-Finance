# Kosashi Capital — Market Making Compliance Policy (internal, v0.1 draft)

Status: DRAFT from MMaaS Layer 5 (2026-11-26). Must be reviewed by a lawyer before client #1.
Applies to: every Kosashi founder, employee, contractor and every client book we run.

## 1. What we do

We provide real, two-sided liquidity so genuine buyers and sellers can trade a token cheaply and smoothly. We create no signal of our own about price, volume or demand.

Test for every action: "If the public knew why we placed this order, would they feel tricked?" If yes, we don't do it.

## 2. Never (no exceptions, regardless of client request or licence status)

1. No wash trading: no trades between accounts controlled by us or by the client to create volume. Self-trade prevention is always on.
2. No volume promises: we never sell, price, or "hypothetically" quote a volume package.
3. No price support: we never hold or defend a price level. We smooth price moves, we never hold price.
4. No pumping: no aggressive one-sided buying to push price, especially before unlocks or announcements.
5. No spoofing or layering: every order is a size we would genuinely fill. No order or ladder larger than the inventory in the account.
6. No painting the tape: no trades timed to shape candles, closing prices or snapshots.
7. No insider trading: client news is used only to prepare liquidity (capacity, ladder, inventory top-ups requested from the client). Never to trade direction or skew to accumulate.
8. No directional programs: we do not run buy or sell programs for clients (accumulation, buybacks, treasury selling). That is outside our service. Clients who need it use an OTC desk, and buybacks must be publicly announced by the client.

## 3. Personal and firm trading

1. Kosashi's own book and all staff never trade any client's token, on any venue, during the engagement and for 30 days after.
2. Client event information is shared only with the people running that client's book.
3. Standard reply to anyone asking about a client token: "I never comment on tokens we work with."

## 4. Custody and access

1. The client owns the account. We accept only trade-only API keys, withdrawals disabled, IP-locked.
2. We never receive client tokens or stablecoins into Kosashi wallets or accounts. If a client finds sub-accounts hard, we help them set one up.
3. Client and Kosashi funds are never mixed.
4. Clients must not trade their own token against the book we run for them (e.g. treasury selling into our bids). Exchanges see the same owner on both sides and flag it as self-trading. Treasury cash-outs go via OTC or a stablecoin withdrawal with 48 h notice.

## 5. Client screening (KYB/KYC) before signing

Required:
- Registered entity and documents; founders' real identities (private KYC under NDA is fine if they are publicly anonymous)
- Sanctions screening of entity and founders
- Whitepaper, real product, tokenomics and full unlock schedule
- Source of the lent inventory (treasury wallet, explained)
- Proof of listing agreements on the venues we will quote

Do not sign until cleared: refused private KYC, founders linked to past rugs, sanctions hit, unverifiable entity.

## 6. Red flags in conversations

- Asks for "volume", "visibility", "momentum", "green candles", "defend the price", "walls"
- Urgent onboarding just before a team or investor unlock
- Wants to pay only in their own token
- Wants to send tokens to our wallet
- "Other MMs do it", "off the record", "no need to write that down", pressure to sign today

Rule: an ask-flag gets one kind explanation of what we do instead. Repeated = we walk away.
Never joke along, never discuss a price for a prohibited service. Assume any call may be recorded.

## 7. Records

1. After every sales call, a short written note: who, what was asked, what we answered (especially any declined request).
2. Keep order, cancel and fill logs for every client book (audit trail), plus deposits, withdrawals and inventory changes.
3. Keep KYB files for every client.

## 8. Contracts

- Default: Singapore law, SIAC arbitration. May concede: another recognised international centre (DIFC, London, Hong Kong). Red line: client's ordinary local courts.
- Payment monthly in advance. Unpaid after the grace period means we stop quoting, then terminate for cause. Arbitration only for large sums.

- Lawyer review of this policy and the term sheet.
